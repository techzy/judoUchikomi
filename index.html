Uchikomi Coach

Harai goshi · live form cues

How to use
1. Prop the phone about 45° to the side of the pole, so your whole body is in frame from head to feet.
2. Tap Start, then stand still in kamae facing the pole until you hear "Go".
3. Drill. You'll get one spoken cue after each rep.
4. Record a reference (you on a good day, or your coach) so your reps are scored against real targets instead of the defaults.
